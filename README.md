# Concerns regarding Neon and their behavior in the AOU community

This issue is primarily about the behavior of **Neon**, rather than the existence of NeonMenu itself.

People are free to develop whatever Among Us mods they want, and people are free to use whichever projects they prefer.

The problem is the repeated toxic, hostile, and trolling behavior that I have experienced from Neon, particularly within the **AOU Discord server**.

## The behavior

Interactions with Neon have repeatedly gone beyond normal disagreements between developers.

Instead of having a normal discussion when someone disagrees with them, asks a question, or brings up another project, the conversation can turn into trolling, hostility, and unnecessary arguments.

This is especially frustrating in a community like AOU, where developers and users should be able to discuss mods without every disagreement turning into personal drama.

People should be able to:

- Ask questions
- Ask for help
- Discuss different mods
- Talk about development
- Disagree with a developer
- Use a different project

without being met with hostility or trolling.

Disagreement is completely normal in open-source development.

Turning every disagreement into an opportunity to provoke or antagonize someone is not.

## Trolling and toxicity

The main issue I have with Neon is the repeated pattern of trolling and toxic interactions.

Rather than simply disagreeing with someone, the behavior can become deliberately provocative and hostile.

This makes ordinary conversations unnecessarily difficult and creates an environment where people have to think twice about saying anything because they may end up getting talked down and shamed.

A developer should be able to receive criticism without immediately turning the discussion into personal hostility.

Likewise, users asking questions or looking for help should not have to deal with someone being unnecessarily aggressive towards them.

If someone asks a question, answer it.

If someone is wrong, explain why.

If someone disagrees with you, explain your position.

If someone prefers another mod, let them use it.

There is no reason for every interaction to become a fight.

## AOU Discord

This is particularly relevant to the **AOU Discord server** because that is where much of this behavior has taken place.

AOU is a community where different Among Us projects, developers, and users interact.

That means disagreements are inevitable.

What should not be normal is repeatedly turning those disagreements into trolling, hostility, or personal attacks.

Nobody should feel like they cannot participate in a public discussion simply because Neon is involved in it.

A community becomes significantly worse when people are afraid to ask questions or express an opinion because they expect to be mocked, provoked, or dragged into an argument.

## This is not about NeonMenu existing

To be clear, I am not asking for NeonMenu to be removed simply because I disagree with Neon.

Neon is free to develop NeonMenu.

People are free to use it.

People are also free to criticize it or choose another project.

The issue is the behavior surrounding the project and the way Neon interacts with other people.

A project should be judged by its code and functionality.

A developer should be judged by how they conduct themselves when interacting with the people around them.

Those are two separate things.

## What should happen instead

There is nothing complicated about this.

If someone wants to discuss NeonMenu, discuss NeonMenu.

If someone reports an issue, address the issue.

If someone asks for help, help them.

If someone disagrees, have a normal discussion.

If someone chooses another mod menu, let them.

Not every disagreement needs to become trolling.

And not every person who disagrees with you needs to be treated like an enemy.

The Among Us modding community is already small enough without developers constantly creating unnecessary hostility between each other.
